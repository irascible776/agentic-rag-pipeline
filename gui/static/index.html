Quick Start Guide

How to use Workbench

Follow these three steps to query and extract grounded insights from your documentation.

1. What to Upload

Add .pdf, .docx, .txt, or .md documents. Ideal for technical specs, research papers, legal contracts, and long-form manuals.

PDF DOCX TXT MD

2. Zero-Friction Indexing

Drag files into the sidebar. Text is extracted, parsed into semantic chunks, and vectorized via Gemini Embeddings into your vector database automatically.

Chunk Size: 1500 chars Overlap: 150 chars

3. Grounded Synthesis

Chat enables immediately once indexed. Answers are strictly synthesized from retrieved passages with transparent source attribution citations.
